Where should the crew stage before calls rise?

One available crew covers Riverside and Eastwood districts. Adjust the storm track or the Northgate lot entrance to see how the plan changes.

How the forecast works

Each hour starts with 8 Riverside calls and 6 Eastwood calls. A fictional storm curve adds a share of the selected track's peak excess, rounded separately for each district. The flood band adds up to 75 and 38 calls respectively. The wind band adds up to 24 and 65. The staging alert threshold is 60 combined calls per hour.
